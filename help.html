Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept reservations?

Yes, we highly recommend making reservations, especially during peak hours. You can call us or email for reservations.

Is there parking available?

Valet parking is available at the restaurant entrance. Street parking is also available in the surrounding area.

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Absolutely! Our menu is entirely vegetarian, and we can accommodate most dietary preferences. Please inform us of any allergies or restrictions when making your reservation.
